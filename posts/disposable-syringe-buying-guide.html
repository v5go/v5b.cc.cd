Disposable Syringe Specifications: A Complete B2B Buying Guide

By V5 Medical Growth Team · Category: Syringes / B2B Sourcing · Oct 14, 2026 · 10 min read
Target readers: Medical device distributors, hospital procurement officers, global wholesalers, tender bidders

Executive Summary

Disposable syringes are among the highest-volume medical consumables in global healthcare procurement. For B2B buyers, the challenge is navigating tip types, barrel designs, volumes, needle configurations, and sterilization standards — while ensuring every lot meets regulatory requirements.

1. Syringe Anatomy

ComponentMaterialKey Spec
BarrelMedical-grade PPTransparent, clear graduation marks
PlungerPPSmooth operation, anti-detachment
GasketLatex or latex-freeLatex-free increasingly required in EU/US
NeedleStainless steel 30416G–30G, EO sterilized

2. Tip Types

Tip TypeConnectionBest For
Luer LockThreaded, screw-onSecure; standard for most injections
Luer SlipFriction-fitQuick attach; low-pressure applications
Catheter TipTapered, larger boreWound irrigation, enteral feeding

Procurement tip: Luer Lock is the default for hospital tenders. When in doubt, stock Luer Lock — it accepts both Luer Lock and Luer Slip needles.

3. Barrel Design: 2-Part vs 3-Part

DesignAdvantages
2-PartLower cost; veterinary/industrial use
3-PartBetter seal; standard for medical use

4. Volume Specifications

VolumeCommon Use
1 mlInsulin, tuberculin, precise dosing
2 ml / 3 mlGeneral injections; most common
5 mlStandard adult injections
10 mlLarger doses, blood draw
20 ml / 50 mlIrrigation, enteral feeding

Stocking strategy: 3ml, 5ml, and 10ml cover 70%+ of hospital demand.

5. Regulatory Checklist

  • ISO 13485 quality system certificate
  • ISO 7886 (sterile hypodermic syringes) compliance
  • CE (EU) / FDA 510(k) (US)
  • EO sterilization validation report
  • Free Sale Certificate (government tenders)

Quality red flag: No ISO 7886 test reports — disqualifying for medical use.

6. FAQ

Q: Luer Lock or Luer Slip?
A: Luer Lock for hospitals. Luer Slip for high-speed vaccination programs.

Q: 2-part or 3-part?
A: 3-part for human medical use. 2-part for veterinary/industrial only.

Q: How to verify EO sterilization?
A: Request the validation report; check EO residual testing per ISO 10993-7.
